# Reviewer Phrasebank

받은 심사평(TFSC EE·리뷰어)과 작성한 심사평에서 추출한 표현 골격. 문장은 원고에 맞게 다시 쓴다.

## 강도별 어조

| 강도 | 표현 |
|---|---|
| 결정적 우려 (reject급) | "I regret that, in its current form, …" / "I am not convinced that the manuscript establishes …" / "My major concern is with …" / "I am deeply concerned about the contribution of this work to the technology management community." |
| 실질 보완 요구 (major) | "The authors have to establish a solid and compelling framework of …" / "I would expect a coherent case study that connects …" / "This requires a dedicated section rather than a brief mention in the conclusions." |
| 권유 (major~minor) | "I would encourage the authors to …" / "It would strengthen the paper if …" / "The authors may wish to reconsider …" |
| 확인 질문 (minor) | "I am not sure whether I missed the details, but …" / "It is unclear which … was used for …" / "Could the authors clarify …?" |
| 형식 (minor) | "Please check the consistency of …" / "Several typographical errors remain (e.g., …)." |

## 축별 상용 골격

**A 기여·포지셔닝**
- "The title highlights X, but most of the content addresses Y."
- "I do not yet see a clear positioning of the method against the literature."
- "The core audience of [journal] are business and management researchers rather than information scientists; the paper should start from [TM problem] rather than from [technique]."
- "The extensibility claims in Section N appear too broad; I suggest narrowing them to the core scope actually demonstrated."
- "Given that patents do not systematically document all technical problems, the output is better framed as a draft for expert refinement than as a final result."

**B 이론**
- "The concept of '…' is introduced without a definition or a link to prior constructs such as …"
- "The equivalence between X and Y is assumed rather than justified."
- "The literature review reads like a technical piece for information science; an introduction to [technology intelligence] — what it is, why it requires patent analysis, what the gap is and why LLMs — would bring the work closer to the journal's audience."

**C 방법 정당화**
- "For each processing task, a single method is adopted without comparison against alternatives (e.g., … vs. …)."
- "The prompting strategy is not validated; a comparison of zero-shot, few-shot, and chain-of-thought variants across the tasks where prompting is applied would be needed."
- "There is an internal tension: the manuscript criticises the opacity of LLMs yet relies on an LLM as the sole evaluator."
- "Applying a macro-level analytical tool to a small, single-country sample raises the question of whether the observed patterns are structural or artefacts of sample size."

**D 검증**
- "Please report inter-rater reliability (e.g., Cohen's κ, ICC) for the gold standard, together with annotator expertise and sampling strategy."
- "An ICC of 0.44 indicates moderate agreement at best; this should be acknowledged as a limitation rather than presented as adequate."
- "The evaluation relies on LLM-as-a-judge alone; agreement with human judgement and checks for position bias are needed."
- "More detail on the two-round expert survey is needed: how it was performed, the questions asked, whether the same experts participated in both rounds, and the level of agreement among them."
- "Sections 6.3 and 6.4 are inconsistent on how many domains were validated; I suggest distinguishing 'validation' (hydrogen storage) from 'demonstration' (HBM, mRNA)."
- "The causal network should be framed as hypothesis-generating rather than validated."

**E 결과**
- "The results section is oriented toward validation rather than storytelling; a coherent case that connects all modules for one technological domain is expected."
- "Interactive communication between the quantitative outputs and qualitative discussion by domain experts is necessary."
- "Which firm filed the patents that anchor cluster N, and do they in fact address the stated problem?"

**F 논의**
- "The discussion does not position the approach against the literature — there is no reference to prior work in the final sections at all."
- "The limits of the approach, the persistence of some subjectivity, and the underlying difficulties (e.g., …) should be discussed more in depth."
- "The solutions retrieved from patents may already exist; the authors should note that novel solutions may require other sources such as scientific publications."
- "Practical implications for potential users should be better considered."

**G 정합성**
- "Cluster counts differ between Table 3 (n = …) and Section 5.2 (n = …)."
- "While Table 2 compares several LLMs, the remainder of the paper does not specify which model was used."
- "'Z-score' and 'Ratio' in Section 4.2.2 are undefined."

**H 응답서 (2차 이상)**
- "The response letter reproduces revised passages that the reviewer reads anyway in the tracked version; a clear indication of what was changed and where would be more helpful than standard expressions of appreciation."
- "I could not locate the corresponding change in the manuscript."

## 국문 학위논문 심사 표현 (~함 체)

- "세 개 하위 연구 간 관계와 각 모듈의 위치·역할을 명확히 할 필요가 있음. 흐름도 수정 필요함."
- "원문 대신 statement 수준으로 재구성해 분석하는 당위성 검토 필요함. 원문 입력과의 비교 실험 필요함."
- "본 연구에서 제시하는 'strategy'의 정의와 구체적 제안 내용을 명시해야 함."
- "LLM-as-a-judge 등 생성형 AI 기반 평가와 전문가 검토를 추가해 신뢰성을 강화할 필요가 있음."
- "설문 결과와 응답자 프로필을 명확히 제시해야 함. 수식 구조·명칭 통일 점검 필요함."
- "연구 기여가 motivation에 포함되어 있어야 함."
- "연구의 확장성이 지나치게 광범위함. 핵심만 명확히 제시할 필요가 있음."
- "기존 classical 방법(전문가·휴리스틱 기반)과의 비교로 신뢰성을 높여야 함. 문제 추출 단계에만 적용한 한계를 명시하고 향후 연구로 언급할 것."

## 피해야 할 표현

- "The paper is interesting/novel/timely" 류의 빈 칭찬
- "The authors should consider" 만 있고 무엇을 어떻게가 없는 문장
- "unclear", "not convincing"으로 끝나는 지적 (요구 조치 없음)
- 등급 단어(reject, major revision)를 본문에 노출
- 인신 비판, 과거 심사에 대한 감정 표현
